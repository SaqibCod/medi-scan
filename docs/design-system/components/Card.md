Rounded container (`radius-lg`, `line` border).

- `surface` (default) for resting content; `glass` (`glass` fill, `blur-glass`, `shadow-panel`) when it floats over the helix or a gradient; `panel` for the large upload panel (`radius-xl`, 36px padding).
- Passing `onClick` turns it into a real `<button>` that lifts and borders in `accent` on hover. `selected` keeps the accent border and a soft tint.
- Never add a coloured left border or an emoji header.
