Classic Tomato Basil Soup

Ingredients:
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 10 fresh basil leaves, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
4. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and vegetable broth.
5. Simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Blend the soup until smooth, then return it to the pot.
7. Stir in the heavy cream and basil.
8. Season with salt and pepper, and serve warm.
